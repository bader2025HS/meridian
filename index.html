What is Athar?

History doesn't disappear.
It leaves traces.

Athar — أَثَر — means trace, mark, imprint. Every border, every ceasefire, every revolution leaves one. This is a place to read those traces clearly: how empires became nation-states, how ideas turned into policy, and how the present keeps arguing with the past.
