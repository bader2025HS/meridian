ATHAR Across Space & Time
539 BCE Babylon

Fall of Babylon

In 539 BCE, Babylon passed into the hands of Cyrus II of Persia, bringing the Neo-Babylonian Empire to an end and incorporating its territories into the Achaemenid Empire.

What happened?

Cyrus II's forces defeated the Babylonian kingdom during a campaign that culminated in the Persian occupation of Babylon. The transition placed the city within a much larger imperial structure extending across Western Asia.

Why does it matter?

The conquest represented a major shift in the political balance of the ancient Near East. Babylon remained an important urban and administrative centre, but power now belonged to an empire whose territorial reach was considerably greater.

The wider picture

The Achaemenid state would continue expanding under Cyrus and his successors, connecting territories from Central Asia and the Iranian plateau to Mesopotamia, Anatolia and Egypt.
