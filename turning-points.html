539 BCE Mesopotamia

Fall of
Babylon

In 539 BCE, Babylon fell to Cyrus the Great, bringing the Neo-Babylonian Empire to an end and incorporating the city into the Persian Empire.

What happened?

Cyrus II of Persia invaded the Neo-Babylonian Empire and defeated its forces. Babylon itself was subsequently taken by Persian forces, apparently with relatively little destruction to the city.

The conquest ended the reign of Nabonidus and transformed Babylon from the centre of an independent empire into one of the major cities of the rapidly expanding Achaemenid Persian Empire.

Why it matters

The fall of Babylon marked a major shift in the political order of the ancient Near East. Cyrus inherited Babylon's territories and institutions while building an empire that would eventually stretch across much of Western and Central Asia.
